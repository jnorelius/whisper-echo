How Whisper Echo decides.

We do not use a public application. A form on the internet turns this into a benefit program. Whisper Echo walks with a small number of families. A person decides that, after a conversation.

When someone asks whether a family “filled out a form,” the answer is no. We sat with them. Then we wrote down what we heard and what we decided. That writing is this record.

  1. Someone makes us aware A family writes, or a pastor, neighbor, or friend does. We do not arrive uninvited. If the family does not know we were contacted, we do not show up.
  2. A person answers The first note only needs a name, a way to reach them, and which season they are in. They do not have to tell the whole story.
  3. We sit with it Jordan or Kelly speaks with them. We are listening for a household that lost a spouse or parent, a season they are already in, and a gap we can actually close. We are not the missing spouse. We are not emergency services. If someone is in immediate danger, that is 911.
  4. We decide honestly Walk with them now, not now, or no. We will say so. We do not take on more families than we can stay with.
  5. We write it down After that conversation, Whisper Echo completes the Family walk record. The family does not apply by filling it. We keep the PDF in a private file.

Reach us

Write us. A person answers.

Whisper Echo walks with widows, widowers, and children after a spouse or parent dies. We are not the missing spouse. We stay through the season they are in.

We walk with a small number of families. We will tell you honestly if we can walk with a family now.

  • The first days Bills, paperwork, funeral costs, a human who answers.
  • This season The life they were already in — a child’s team, a repair, a mortgage.
  • The lasting piece When there is money, help turning it into a legacy. We do not give legal advice. We pay vetted attorneys.

If this is your family

In the first note, your name, a way to reach you, and which season you are in is enough. You do not have to tell the whole story.

If you know a family

A pastor, a neighbor, a friend — that is often how we first hear. We do not arrive uninvited.

Tell us your name, how you know them, and whether they know you are writing. Do not send their whole story. We will not use your note to surprise a family.

Make us aware of a family

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. Whisper Echo is not emergency services.
